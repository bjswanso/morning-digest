Section 2 — Personal Care

**1. Mental Health — The 5-4-3-2-1 Reset (5 min)**

Before your first meeting of the day, run a quick sensory grounding drill: name 5 things you can see, 4 you can touch, 3 you can hear, 2 you can smell, 1 you can taste. It's the cognitive equivalent of rebooting a system before loading a heavy program — clears background anxiety and sharpens executive function. Brains that manage both a P&L and a thesis defense benefit from the occasional hard refresh.

**2. Relationship — The Specific Appreciation Note (5 min)**

Write — not text, *write* — one sentence to your spouse that names something specific they did this week and why it mattered to you. ("You handled the grocery run without being asked while I was on that 7 a.m. call, and it meant I could actually focus.") Specificity is the signal that you were paying attention. Generalized gratitude is wallpaper; precise gratitude is architecture.

**3. Science/Culture — One Counterintuitive Finding (10 min)**

Pull up a recent abstract from *Nature Human Behaviour* or *Science* and read just the abstract and conclusions — not the full paper. The goal isn't mastery; it's pattern exposure. As someone building AI fluency in a domain-expert role, regularly dipping into adjacent scientific literature is how you cross-pollinate. Bonus: it gives you exactly the kind of unexpected data point that makes INTJ small talk tolerable for everyone.
