**1. Physical Health — The Two-Minute Posture Reset (≤5 min)**

Years at a standing desk are still years of forward-head posture in disguise. Set a timer right now: do 10 chin tucks, 10 shoulder blade squeezes, and a 60-second doorway chest stretch. That's it. The research on desk workers is unambiguous — cumulative micro-breaks outperform one long stretch at day's end. Think of it as defragging your spine before the day's files get corrupted.

**2. Gratitude — The Specificity Rule (≤5 min)**

Generic gratitude ("I'm grateful for my health") is the cognitive equivalent of a form letter — your brain files it and moves on. Instead, write down one hyper-specific thing from the past 24 hours: a phrase someone said, a moment of quiet, a problem that clicked into place. Specificity is what converts gratitude from a platitude into an actual mood-altering neurological event. Pen and paper preferred; your brain takes handwriting more seriously than it does a notes app.
