Section 2 — Personal Care

**1. Physical Health (≤15 min) — Micro-movement reset**

Between back-to-back calendar blocks, do one 10-minute standing stretch sequence targeting the neck, shoulders, and hip flexors — the exact joints that calcify during long stretches of senior-manager-meets-grad-student sitting. Think of it as a system reboot: your body runs the same OS whether you're in a board meeting or a seminar, and it ships no firmware updates without movement.

**2. Relationship with Spouse (≤15 min) — The 2-minute appreciation ritual**

Before dinner tonight, tell your spouse one specific, concrete thing they did in the last 48 hours that made your life easier or better — not a general compliment, but a named observation. Research in positive psychology consistently shows that *specific* appreciation lands with greater emotional weight than generic praise. For an INTJ, precision is already a strength; deploy it toward your partner. It costs 120 seconds and compounds over time like a well-indexed fund.

**3. Happiness / Culture (≤15 min) — Intentional creative input**

Queue up one piece of music, a two-minute architectural photograph series, or a short poem by a creator whose cultural background differs from your own — and give it your full, uninterrupted attention. No multitasking. Cognitive science calls this "aesthetic engagement": brief, focused exposure to unfamiliar creative work measurably expands perspective and lifts mood. Consider it cross-training for the mind you're already investing so much in.
