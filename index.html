Personal Care

**1. Physical Health — 5-Minute Morning Reset (≤10 min)**

Before your first meeting or screen glance, do 10 minutes of Zone 2 movement — a brisk walk, light cycling, or even marching in place. Think of it as defragging your operating system before loading any applications. At 50, consistent low-intensity cardio is one of the highest-ROI investments you can make in cognitive clarity and metabolic health. No equipment, no commute required.

**2. Gratitude — Specificity Sprint (≤5 min)**

Open a notes app and write three things you're grateful for — but impose a constraint: each must be specific enough that no one else on Earth could have written it (e.g., not "my health," but "the fact that I caught the error in slide 14 before the board saw it"). Specificity is the difference between gratitude as a platitude and gratitude as a neural pattern. INTJs tend to dismiss vague sentiment; precision makes it stick.

**3. Strengthening a Relationship with Your Spouse — The 6-Second Deposit (≤2 min)**

Research by relationship scientists suggests that the quality of greetings and departures disproportionately shapes relationship satisfaction. Before you both pivot to your Saturday tasks, make eye contact, put the phone down, and give a genuine 6-second hello — long enough to feel intentional, short enough to survive a busy morning. Treat it like the handshake at the start of a negotiation: sets the entire tone of what follows.
