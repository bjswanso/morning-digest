Section 2 — Personal Care

**1. Physical Health — Move for 10 minutes before your first meeting.**

Resistance to morning exercise is mostly an activation energy problem, not a willpower problem. Put on shoes, set a 10-minute timer, and do a circuit of bodyweight squats, push-ups, and a brisk walk. You're not training for an Ironman; you're just priming your dopamine system before the calendar colonizes your prefrontal cortex. Done.

**2. Strengthening a Relationship with Your Spouse — Give a specific, unprompted compliment today.**

General compliments ("you're great") metabolize quickly. Specific ones compound. Think of one thing your spouse did in the past 72 hours that you noticed but didn't say out loud — then say it, in a single sentence, before 9 a.m. The return on that 30-second investment will outlast your morning coffee.

**3. Gratitude — Write the three best things about yesterday.**

Not three things that *went* well — three things that were genuinely good, however small. Cognitive science calls this a "positive data log," and it essentially re-trains your INTJ pattern-recognition engine to scan for signal instead of defaulting to gap analysis. Takes under three minutes; the compound interest is real.
