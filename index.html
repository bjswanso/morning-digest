Section 2 — Personal Care

Three suggestions, completable in 15 minutes or less. Today's mix: **physical health**, **strengthening a relationship**, and **gratitude**.

**1. Physical Health — The 10-Minute Reset (aka Minimum Effective Dose)**

Step outside for a brisk 10-minute walk — no podcast, no agenda. Morning light exposure within the first hour of waking suppresses melatonin, sharpens alertness, and nudges your circadian clock in a way no amount of office fluorescence can replicate. Think of it as rebooting the operating system before loading any applications.

**2. Strengthening a Relationship with Your Spouse — The Specific Appreciation Drill**

Research on relationship satisfaction consistently finds that *specific* appreciation outperforms generic praise by a wide margin. Set a 5-minute timer and write or say three things you genuinely admire about your spouse right now — not "you're supportive," but *why* and *when*. Precision is the love language of people who think in systems. Specificity signals that you actually paid attention.

**3. Gratitude — The Contrast Technique**

For 5 minutes, write down one current circumstance you're grateful for, then briefly note what its absence would look like. The contrast — what psychologists call "mental subtraction" — consistently produces stronger gratitude than simple listing, because it converts an abstraction into a felt reality. Your master's program, your career, your health: subtract one, feel the weight of it, then set the pen down and carry that lightness into the day.
