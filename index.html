Section 2 — Personal Care

**1. Physical health — The 10-minute joint reset**

After your morning coffee and before your laptop opens, spend 10 minutes on "floor time": seated spinal rotations, hip circles, and a doorway chest opener. Sustained desk work and long video calls load the same posture patterns for hours; this brief sequence interrupts that groove before it sets. Think of it as a version control commit — small, frequent saves beat one catastrophic restore. Set a phone timer so the overachiever in you doesn't negotiate it down to two.

**2. Relationship — The curiosity question**

Text or tell your spouse one genuinely curious question today — not logistical ("Did you call the plumber?"), but something that opens a window: *"What's something you've been quietly thinking about lately?"* Research on long-term partnerships consistently shows that curiosity, not grand gestures, is the compound interest of intimacy. Costs zero dollars and about 45 seconds to send. The conversation it starts is the ROI.

**3. Gratitude — The specificity practice**

Before your first meeting, write down one thing you're grateful for — but with a rule: no abstractions. Not *"my health"* but *"that I can walk to the coffee machine without pain."* Not *"my team"* but *"that Maya pushed back on my slide deck and was right."* Specificity is the difference between a form letter and a handwritten note. The brain responds accordingly: vague gratitude is processed and filed; precise gratitude actually lands.
